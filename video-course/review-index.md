Learn inserts connect sessions, retrieval, skills and both approval mechanisms to the repository. Deliberate frame holds are labeled; captured mouse movement remains at its original speed. Lab 02 retains the corrected debugger sequence and branded exercise card, with excess silence removed. Browser history/navigation leaks were cut or redacted, and incidental editor account/profile information is concealed before cropping or holding footage. Captions, chapters and timed scripts follow the revised cuts. See the revision report for scope and verification limits.

Use `lab number — video timestamp — requested change`. The timed script provides scene ranges, and each `shots.json` plus `teaching-edits.json` identifies the source recording and current editorial overrides. Read the lab's expected result and recovery exercise when assessing whether a learner can reproduce the lesson. Embedded subtitles can be enabled in VLC; SRT and VTT files are also beside every MP4.
