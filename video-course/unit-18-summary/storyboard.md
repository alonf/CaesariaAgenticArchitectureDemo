# Closing chapter — the journey and your next step

Course: **Agentic C#**, by Alon Fliess. This is the conclusion after Lab 17, not another runtime stage or Director beat. Target 9–11 minutes; final timing follows the narration. Preserve the accepted course and reuse selected, privacy-reviewed footage rather than reenacting the entire lab series.

## Learner outcome

The learner can explain the architecture journey and write a first design brief for either an agentic layer over an existing C# application or a new C# agentic system. The brief identifies the authoritative service, agent responsibility, context, permission boundary, failure behavior and independent evidence of completion.

## Story arc and shooting decisions

| Beat | Screen | Why this belongs in the conclusion |
|---|---|---|
| Look how far you have come | Earlier daylight anomaly, then journey map | Return to the familiar incident and show the change in the learner's understanding. Explicitly identify separate recorded experiments. |

| Make progress and failure observable | Actual durable review, worker interruption, unchanged review, terminal evidence | Separate conversation continuity from business-process recovery. Only local worker recovery was demonstrated. |

## Production rules

- Use the approved signature background, large text, and actual deck/app thumbnails where they reinforce the point. Full-frame footage; no permanent top/bottom title strips.
- Excerpts are retrospective and have their original fixture/reset boundaries. Never imply all results came from one continuous run or a new execution.
- New narration replaces source audio. Phrase anchors control cuts; holds are explicitly labeled. Existing source highlights are retained only where they point to the evidence being discussed; inspect every reused excerpt with the new narration.
- Reuse only the reviewed final exports recorded in `sources.json`, including hashes. Their browser/editor privacy redactions are already embedded. No new account or cloud screen is recorded for this chapter.
- Say Microsoft Agent Framework or Agent Framework, never the acronym. Keep synthetic narration and rough-cut status in the companion notes/file metadata.
- The source book is the rationale recorded in [book alignment](../book-alignment.md). Current repository behavior and recorded evidence take precedence over an aspirational storyboard statement.
